FAQ

Frequently asked.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Flexible, depending on the mix of fits and sizes. We work with small boutiques and large distributors. Message us on WhatsApp with your requirements.

Which sizes do you offer?

Sizes 16 to 40. Availability varies by fit; see the size guide.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. We work with wholesale partners worldwide and can arrange international shipping for bulk orders.

Standard orders follow our regular production cycle; repeat orders of existing designs are typically faster. We discuss timelines at the start.
