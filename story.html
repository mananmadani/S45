Our mission

Jeans first, always.

Most clothing brands make jeans. S45 is a jeans brand. When jeans are everything, there are no compromises on fabric, fit or finish.

We make only boys' denim and always have. Every decision asks: is this the best we can do for a boy who wears jeans every day?

"The finest boys' jeans, built without compromise."
What we stand for

Three principles, no exceptions.

01

Craftsmanship

One standard for every pair. Quality is the baseline, never a premium feature.

  • Triple-stitched pockets and belt loops
  • Inspected piece by piece
  • Consistent colour across batches
02

Fit science

A boy's size 28 is not a shrunk adult 28. Each fit is built on age-appropriate blocks.

  • Separate toddler, child and teen grading
  • Every fit tested across sizes 16–40
  • Rise and waist calibrated by age
03

Scale with integrity

Growth has never been an excuse to lower quality. Every batch meets the first pair's standard.

  • 200+ genuinely distinct designs
  • Partners chosen for brand alignment
  • Capacity grown without trade-offs
Design philosophy

Built for real boys.

Design starts from the body, not a trend board: what does a boy of this age and size actually need?

  • Pocket depth and placement designed for use
  • Waistbands built for each age group's proportions
  • Fabric weight chosen for season and fit
  • Washes selected for longevity and fade resistance
Manufacturing

Made in Ahmedabad.

Close to the finest denim mills and cut-and-sew units, we control every step from fabric selection to dispatch. No middle layer: when something needs attention, we see it and fix it.

  • Manufactured & marketed by Ratan Fashion
  • Mills chosen for consistency and colour fastness
  • Every batch inspected before release
